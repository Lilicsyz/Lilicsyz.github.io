🎥 MASTERS OF HORROR

THE FOUNDER

✎
1939 — 2015
WES CRAVEN
Father of modern horror. He not only created Freddy Krueger but also gave the world Ghostface. His vision and love for the genre made "Scream" a revolution in slashers.

THE NEW WAVE

✎
2026 (debut as director)
KEVIN WILLIAMSON
Creator of the "Scream" universe. He wrote the scripts for the first two films, setting the tone for the entire franchise. In 2026, he returns as the director of the seventh chapter.
✎
2022 — present
MATT BETTINELLI-OLPIN
✎
2022 — present
TYLER GILLETT
The Radio Silence duo. Brought "Scream" back to life after Wes Craven's passing. Their work (parts 5 and 6) continued the legacy with love and respect, adding modern style and new rules.
